Wroth

Wroth is a fast area control game for 2–4 players (or 1–4 cooperatively against an AI faction). Each faction drafts shared action dice, floods the central plateau with troops, and spends Corra on elite troops and faction powers to hold the most valuable regions.

Objective

Score victory points by controlling regions. The game ends after any round in which a player reaches 30 VP; the highest total wins. Ties go to whoever controls more regions (not counting the Shores).

Game Setup

  1. Map: Place the map and VP tracker in the center. Each player picks a deployment side.
  2. Action Dice: Use 7 action dice for 2–3 players or 9 for 4 players.
  3. VP Tokens: Place a random VP token on each numbered region.
  4. Factions: Each player takes a faction playmat, its basic troops and elite dice, an All-Seeing Die, and one of the faction's three feat cards.
  5. Deploy: Each player places three basic troops in their deployment zone.

Round Structure

  1. Maintenance: Advance the round counter and pass the First Player token (skipped in round one).
  2. Spoils: In region order, whoever has the most troops in a region takes the bonus action shown on its VP token (if any). Ties get nothing.
  3. Groundwork: Everyone deploys two basic troops to their deployment zone, rolls their All-Seeing Die, and takes one Corra.
  4. Draft: Roll all action dice. Draft clockwise until each player has two (three in a 2-player game). The player with the fewest VP takes the leftover die.
  5. Actions: Clockwise, each player spends one drafted die per turn until all dice are used.
  6. Victory Points: Score each region.

Actions Overview

  • Mine: Gain 1 Corra.
  • Extend: Deploy up to 2 basic troops to a region where you already have troops.
  • Maneuver: Move up to 2 troops to an adjacent region, or deploy 2 basic troops to your deployment zone.
  • Battle: Deal 1 damage in a region you occupy or one adjacent to it.
  • All-Seeing Die (free, once per round): Mine, Strike (1 damage anywhere), Assault (2 damage nearby), or Double (repeat the action you just took).
  • Exodus: Spend two unused dice to move any number of troops from one region into adjacent regions.
  • Corra: Spend at any time on your turn to buy elite troops or trigger faction abilities.

Combat

Damage is split among one opposing faction's troops in the target region. Basic troops die to 1 damage. Elite troops have vitality equal to their die's face; each hit lowers it, and at zero they return to your mat to be bought again. The Shores can't be attacked, but troops that leave the Shores can never go back.

Scoring

  • Control: Most troops in a region scores its VP token value. Ties score nothing.
  • Domination: +1 VP if you are the only faction in a region you control.
  • Elite Bonuses: Some elites add VP in their region (each elite type counts once per region).

Strategy Tips

  • Break ties, don't chase majorities: Tied regions score zero for everyone. One extra troop that turns a tie into control is often worth more than piling into a region you already own.
  • Draft to deny: The dice pool is shared. If an opponent needs a Battle die to flip a key region, taking it can be stronger than taking the die you want most.
  • Leave the Shores deliberately: Troops on the plateau can never return to safety. Commit them where they can score for several rounds rather than trickling them out.
  • Hold Spoils regions: Regions whose tokens show action symbols give a free action every round. Controlling one or two compounds into a big tempo lead.
  • Elites are anchors: An elite soaks several damage, making a region expensive to contest. Buy them for high-value regions you plan to keep, not for one-turn fights.
  • Save the All-Seeing Die: Double after a strong Extend or Assault late in the Action phase can swing a region right before scoring, when opponents have no dice left to answer.
  • Mind the catch-up die: The trailing player gets an extra die each round. Near 30 VP, expect the field to gang up on the leader and spread out to avoid a single decisive Assault.
