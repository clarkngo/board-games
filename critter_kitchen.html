Critter Kitchen

Critter Kitchen is a simultaneous worker-placement game for 1–5 players. You secretly send three critter chefs (a fast Mouse, a middling Lizard, and a slow but strong Boar) to market locations to gather ingredients, spices, and soup, then cook meals for challenges and a final food critic.

Objective

Earn the most stars over 7 rounds from challenge meals (end of rounds 3 and 6), the critic meal (end of round 7), rumours, and end-game bonuses.

Game Setup

  1. Waterfront: Lay out the board and the locations in order: Soup Truck, Garden Top Shop, Desert Vendor, Water Market (3+), Air Docks (4+), Lantern Lane (5), Midnight Merchant, and Chef Academy.
  2. Supplies: Place the bisque and soup tokens on the Soup Truck, one Zouz-chef at the Academy, and the ingredient tokens in the bag.
  3. Challenges & Critic: Lay 6 challenge cards face down, one critic card face up (the Mouse is best for a first game), and 3 rumours face down.
  4. Players: Each player takes a kitchen board, shield, critic and challenge plates, one soup token, and the Mouse, Lizard, and Boar chefs.

Round Structure

  1. Setup: Reveal a challenge card, restock the locations (except the Midnight Merchant), and reveal a new Zouz-chef.
  2. Allocate: Everyone secretly assigns a location card to each chef (one chef per location), then reveals.
  3. Resolve: Locations resolve left to right. At each one, the fastest chef picks first: Mouse takes 1 item, Lizard 2, Boar 3. A chef reaching an empty location gets 1 soup.
  4. Store: Hide your goods behind your shield; rumour tokens go on your kitchen board.
  5. End of Round: Leftovers shift down the line, Zouz-chefs expire, and in rounds 3 and 6 everyone cooks challenge meals.

Key Concepts

  • Midnight Merchant: Its stock is drawn from the bag only when it resolves, so it's a gamble.
  • Chef Academy: Win a Zouz-chef to send a fourth chef next round.
  • Spices: Pair with one matching ingredient to double its value.
  • Soup & Bisque: Substitute for any ingredient in a challenge meal (a meal still needs one real ingredient), and count toward the soup star.
  • Refrigerator: After round 3 you may keep 5 tokens on your critic plate, and 10 after round 6. The rest is discarded.

Scoring

  • Challenge meals: 1 star at 6+ value, 2 at 12+, 4 at 21+.
  • End-game stars: Most soup value, a complete set of all seven ingredients, best value in each course, rumours, and critic or Restaurateur bonuses. Tied players all earn the star.
  • Critic meal: Total your critic plate on the kitchen board's scoring rows for more stars.

Strategy Tips

  • Match chef to location: Send the Mouse where there's one great item you must have. Send the Boar to well-stocked locations that rivals are unlikely to strip first.
  • Read your opponents: Everyone places in secret. Look at what each player needs for revealed challenges and expect them to target it.
  • Empty-handed isn't a total loss: An empty location still gives soup, which is a wild ingredient and builds toward the soup star.
  • Grab Zouz-chefs at the right time: An extra chef is strongest in rounds 3 and 6, right before challenge meals, and in round 7.
  • Hit the 21 threshold: The jump from 2 to 4 stars at 21 value is huge. Pairing spices with high-value ingredients is the quickest way there.
  • Plan the fridge: The 5- and 10-token storage limits are tight. Save premium ingredients and spices for the critic meal, and cook with the rest.
  • Collect toward the full set: One of each of the seven ingredients earns a star at the end. Pick up the missing types when they're cheap.
  • Track rumours: Rumour tokens reveal secret scoring. Learn rumours early to steer toward the ones that pay.
