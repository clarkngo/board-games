Century: Golem Edition

A game where players act as crystal traders, collecting and upgrading crystals to acquire Golem Point Cards. The player with the most points at the end of the game wins.

Player Turn (Perform one of four actions)

Players take turns clockwise. On your turn, you must perform one of the following actions:

  • Work (Play a Merchant Card): Play a Merchant Card from your hand and execute its effect (gain, upgrade, or trade crystals).
  • Acquire a Merchant Card: Take one of the face-up Merchant Cards.
  • Rest: Take all Merchant Cards you have played back into your hand.
  • Claim a Golem Point Card: If you have the required crystals, you may claim a Golem Point Card.

End Game & Scoring

The game ends when a player claims their 6th Golem Point Card (in a 2-3 player game) or 5th Golem Point Card (in a 4-5 player game). The current round is finished, allowing all players an equal number of turns.

Points are calculated from your Golem Point Cards, bonus tokens, and any non-yellow crystals remaining in your caravan.

The player with the most points wins.

Strategy Tips

  • Build an engine early: Acquire a few strong merchant cards in the first several turns. Upgrade and trade cards drive the whole game.
  • Watch the crystal pile on cards: Taking a merchant card further down the row costs crystals but may carry crystals on it. Cheap leftover cards can be free income.
  • Plan rests: Resting costs a turn. Play your best cards in an order that sets up a claim before you need to rest.
  • Mind the 10-crystal cap: Your caravan holds 10 crystals. Upgrade instead of hoarding yellows, and convert to the colors golem cards need.
  • Grab coin bonuses: The leftmost golem cards carry bonus tokens. Claiming them early is extra points.
  • Race the end: The game ends at 5 or 6 golems. Track how close others are and pivot to cheaper golems if the end is near.
  • Leftover crystals score: Non-yellow crystals are worth 1 point each at the end. Use your last turns to upgrade rather than wasting them.
