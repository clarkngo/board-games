Botany

Botany (Dux Somnium Games) is a Victorian plant-hunting adventure. You travel the world on expeditions, collect live and pressed specimens, survive events along the way, and bring your finds home to build a celebrated garden. Includes a solo mode against Lady Abigail Inglesby.

Objective

Score the most points from deposited specimens, garden features, and bonus scoring cards to win the Queen's Prize in Botany. Ties go to the player with more coins.

Game Setup

  1. Board & Decks: Place the board in the center. Set out the Expedition, Event, and Specimen decks face down, and lay 3 Expedition cards face up beside the board.
  2. Bonus Scoring: Reveal 3 Bonus Scoring cards for end-game scoring.
  3. Players: Each player takes an Estate board, Wardian Case, Botanical Press, 10 coins, a random Character card, and 2 Expedition cards.
  4. Pawns: Put one pawn on the Reputation Tracker at 1 and one on your Estate space.
  5. Specimens: Deal each player 4 hidden Specimen cards. Each player may discard any number once and redraw.
  6. First Player: Highest die roll goes first; play continues clockwise.

Turn Structure

  1. Pay 1 coin from your Expedition Fund. If you can't, you must head home.
  2. Draw an Event (optional, but required before you can acquire a specimen that turn). Roll a die, add your skill bonuses, and apply the result.
  3. Move up to 3 spaces over land and sea, acquiring specimens on spaces that match cards in your hand.
  4. Refill your Specimen hand to 4.
  5. Collect Estate Income based on reputation (1, 2, or 3 coins).
  6. Buy at most one card, before your Event or at the end of your turn.

Specimens

  • Live: Stored in your Wardian Case (3 slots). Worth their printed value once deposited.
  • Pressed: Stored in your Botanical Press (no limit). Always worth 1 point.
  • Poisonous: Negative values; pressing one makes it worth 1 point instead.

Returning to the Estate

  • Deposit live specimens for 1 coin each, plus 1 reputation for every 3 deposited together.
  • Move Estate Income into your Expedition Fund, cycle Specimen cards, and swap a face-up Expedition card.
  • Buy one Garden Feature (most need a minimum number of live specimens) for points and sometimes reputation.
  • Pay 1 coin to slip a poisonous specimen onto a rival's Estate.

End of the Game

The game ends when a player reaches 12 reputation or the Specimen deck runs out. Every other player gets one final turn. Specimens not yet deposited score nothing.

Strategy Tips

  • Plan routes, not single plants: Keep specimen cards that are near each other so one trip fills your case. Discard far-flung cards while at the Estate.
  • Return with full cases: Reputation comes in threes, so arrive home with 3 live specimens at a time to maximize reputation, which drives your income.
  • Budget the trip home: Every turn costs a coin. Running dry forces a slow retreat and card discards, so turn back while you can still afford it.
  • Press smartly: Press low-value or poisonous specimens to free case space. Save the case for high-value live plants and Garden Feature requirements.
  • Upgrade your carrying capacity: Extra Wardian Cases and features like the Orangery or Conservatory let each expedition score more.
  • Build skills for Events: Expedition and Character bonuses to the skills you'll test most often (sea routes, for example) turn risky events into rewards.
  • Chase the Bonus cards: The 3 public Bonus Scoring cards are worth steering toward all game, and they show you what rivals are likely to compete for.
  • Control the clock: If you're ahead, race toward 12 reputation. If you're behind, slow down and build bigger garden scores.
