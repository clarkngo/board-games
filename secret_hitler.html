Secret Hitler

A social deduction game for 5-10 players where participants are secretly divided into two teams: Liberals and Fascists, with one player being Secret Hitler.

Roles and Objectives

  • Liberals: Enact 5 Liberal Policies OR assassinate Hitler.
  • Fascists: Enact 6 Fascist Policies OR elect Hitler as Chancellor after 3 Fascist Policies have been enacted.
  • Hitler: Plays for the Fascist team.

Gameplay (Rounds)

Each round consists of three phases: Election, Legislative Session, and Executive Action.

  1. Election: The President nominates a Chancellor, and all players vote.
  2. Legislative Session: The President draws 3 policies, discards one, and passes two to the Chancellor. The Chancellor enacts one policy.
  3. Executive Action: If a Fascist Policy is enacted, the President gains a special power.

Game End

  • Liberal Victory: 5 Liberal Policies enacted OR Hitler is assassinated.
  • Fascist Victory: 6 Fascist Policies enacted OR Hitler is elected Chancellor after 3 Fascist Policies have been enacted.

Strategy Tips

  • Liberals: trust the numbers: Liberals outnumber Fascists. Use voting records and policy claims to find inconsistencies.
  • Track who passes what: Write down each government and what was enacted. Repeated Fascist policies from the same pair are a red flag.
  • Fascists: blend in: Pass Liberal policies early to build trust. The real goal is getting Hitler into the Chancellorship after 3 Fascist policies.
  • Hitler: be boringly liberal: Act like a cautious Liberal. Avoid suspicion so you can be elected Chancellor late.
  • Use executive powers carefully: Investigations and executions are powerful. As a Liberal, use them to confirm or remove likely Fascists.
  • Beware after 3 Fascist policies: Every Chancellor vote becomes dangerous. Only elect players you've confirmed are not Hitler.
  • Don't block too long: Three failed elections in a row enact the top policy at random. Sometimes a risky government is better than chaos.
